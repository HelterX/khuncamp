# Step 3 - Produce

## Script / talking points
new packaging idea (title) + transcript of competitor = script / talking points

Example title rewrites (client's existing topic -> rewritten):

| Client's existing topic | Rewritten |
|---|---|
| Is Real Estate a Good Side Hustle? | NEVER Try Real Estate As a Side Hustle Until You Know This |
| Why You Keep Failing the Real Estate Exam | 87% of People Fail the Real Estate Exam For This One Reason |
| Online vs In Person Real Estate School | I've Trained 1,000 Agents. This School Choice Ruins Most of Them |
| Can You Get Your License With NO Experience? | What Nobody Tells You Before Getting a Real Estate License |
| What It's Really Like After You Get Your License | NEVER Get Your Real Estate License Until You Understand This |
| 5 Sales Careers That Pay Over $100,000 | 5 Jobs That Pay More Than Being a Realtor (I've Done Both) |
| 5 High Paying Jobs With a Real Estate License | Your Real Estate License Is Worth More Doing This Instead |

(Rewrites transcribed from a screenshot. Check wording against the original.)

Prompt:

> Based on the title of our new video for our client [INSERT TITLE]
>
> And the patterns, language, formatting and overall structure and tonality of the competitor's video: [INSERT TRANSCRIPT]
>
> Write the script & talking points for our client.
>
> Use the https://github.com/blader/humanizer skill as a final pass before handing off the script.

The example output starts with a cold open ("You can absolutely do real estate as a side hustle. People do it every day...").

## Thumbnail
new packaging idea (thumbnail) + thumbnail of competitor = reference image + new prompt

Prompt (image model):

> First reference image is our subject, use it to create a new youtube thumbnail.
>
> Second image is an example used as pure reference for the composition, lighting and structure.
>
> Incorporate the following elements into our image as well:
>
> [Can use voice memo to describe exactly what we want to see.]

Example:
- Original: "REAL ESTATE SIDE HUSTLE? DON'T DO IT!" for "Is Real Estate a Good Side Hustle?"
- New and improved: "PART-TIME TRAP" for "NEVER Try Real Estate As a Side Hustle Until You Know This"
