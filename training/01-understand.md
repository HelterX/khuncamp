# Step 1 - Understand

## 1. What they have now (the client)
Analyze the client's content:
- Upload frequency
- Titles
- Thumbnails
- Transcripts

## 2. Where they want to be (competitors)
Find 1-3 competitors and analyze the same four items:
- Upload frequency
- Titles
- Thumbnails
- Transcripts

## 3. Compare them with AI

Prompt (attach screenshots and uploaded materials):

> Based on the screenshots and uploaded materials conduct a comparison between our new client [Name] and the competitors.
>
> There's a clear difference between packaging in titles, thumbnails and overall positioning.
>
> Your job is to deeply understand the patterns as to why our client struggles with growth and also identify the title structures, packaging formulas of the competitors.
>
> Reverse engineer the titles of the competitors and provide a step by step execution system which we can implement into our client's content system.
