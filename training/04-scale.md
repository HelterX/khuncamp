# Step 4 - Scale

We produced the first content piece for the new client. Now we do it at scale, with a client portal hub inside Notion (example: "Seth Dyer - $ynergy Brand $caling").

## Questions to ask
- Upload cadence? Aim for 2x/week depending on complexity.

## Recommended structure
1. **Script / plan / ideate on ONE DAY** (ex. Monday)
   - Consult the client on the ideas, whether they make sense for them. Alignment is key.
   - At this point the title and thumbnail variants should be READY before any other production starts.
2. **Batch recording**
   - The client records the 2 videos on the same day and hands the raw footage to the team on the same day (ex. Tuesday).
   - Efficiency is key, so we do one recording session for BOTH videos.
3. **Editing / finalizing in the next 48 hours** (ex. Wednesday - Thursday)
   - Get one final revision from the client before the end of the week. Keep revisions under 2.
   - Realistically there should be NONE. It's not the client's business to tell us what visuals to have or what song plays in the background.
4. **Scheduling ALWAYS happens in advance.**
   - Never post videos as soon as they are done. Follow a strict posting schedule, with videos paced 2-3 days apart.

## Example timeline
- Week 1: getting started, produce first raw batch.
- Week 2: first week's videos post on Tuesday and Friday. Same workflow as week 1, another production cycle.
- Week 3: after 14 days, read the YouTube data (CTR, AVD, traffic sources, demographics, etc.) and optimize from there.

## Notion client portal (from the screenshot)

- Next Tasks (weekly): daily tracker + calendar/timeline
- Checkpoints: current position, goals 1-5 (find unique "wins", consistent long form traffic, automated repurposing, value assets / lead magnets, live streaming schedule), end goal (onboarding blueprint, Level 1 Traffic, Level 2 Acquisition, Level 3 Fulfillment, Continuity Program)
